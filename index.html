About us

Unique Foundation School is a secondary school serving learners from JSS 1 through SS 3. We value discipline, curiosity and character, and aim to help every student do their best in and beyond the classroom.

Our mission

To provide students with a sound education grounded in strong values.

Our vision

To prepare graduates to contribute with integrity in Nigeria and beyond.

Our values

Integrity, excellence, respect and service.
